What can be inspected

  • Input: download the original image.
  • Recorded prompt: “the Mixue Bingcheng mascot riding on a motorcycle”.
  • Outputs: the two image files preserved in this repository and shown below.

What is not known

The original record does not establish the exact API model IDs, dates, seeds, steps, guidance values, masks, output settings, or whether more than one result was generated before these examples were selected. Those details are therefore not reconstructed or guessed.

How to interpret this page

  • Use it to inspect composition, text rendering, subject replacement, and obvious artifacts.
  • Do not use it to rank model quality, speed, cost, or consistency.
  • A future controlled benchmark should pin the model IDs, preserve raw responses, disclose every run, and publish the selection rule.
